I build end-to-end systems that turn messy real-world inputs — case files, match videos, clinical footage — into structured, auditable outputs. My work sits where **computer vision** and **LLM agents** meet: vision measures the physical world, language models organise the evidence, and deterministic rules own every conclusion that matters.

我关注计算机视觉与大模型智能体的结合，把案卷、比赛视频、检验影像这类非结构化输入，做成可复核、可追溯的结构化结论。

## Selected projects

### [ForenAgent](https://github.com/Sabersmaster9/ForenAgent)

A multi-agent research prototype for forensic clinical disability assessment. Local-first OCR/LLM extraction, human-confirmed goniometer readings, and a deterministic rule engine for ROM loss, disability clauses and recovery periods.  
`Python` `PaddleOCR` `LLM agents` `Rule engine`

### [tennis-trace-1](https://github.com/Sabersmaster9/tennis-trace-1)

Tennis match analysis from video: TrackNet ball tracking, 14-keypoint court detection, CatBoost bounce prediction and player tracking.  
`Python` `PyTorch` `OpenCV` `TrackNet`

### [pingpong-test](https://github.com/Sabersmaster9/pingpong-test)

A one-click Streamlit benchmark for comparing LLM accuracy on a table-tennis question set, with connectivity probes, retry/backoff and dual accuracy metrics.  
`Python` `Streamlit` `LLM evaluation`

### [AI-Powered Intelligent Forensic Identification System](https://github.com/Sabersmaster9/AI-Powered-Intelligent-Forensic-Identification-System) · archived

The earlier MMPose + RAG prototype behind ForenAgent: a machine-vision SOP for joint measurement, with 97.11% joint-tracking accuracy at 2.41° mean angular error.  
`MMPose` `RAG` `Computer vision`
